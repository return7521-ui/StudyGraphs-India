Essential Festival Guidelines & Rules

Everything you need to know about transport, discipline, device allowances, and event scheduling prior to submitting your registration.

🚌

Transport & Timings

On event days, official school buses carry participants from school to Seth Anandram Jaipuria School (Kanpur) and return by ~8:00 PM.

  • After Return: Students must arrange home transport from school.
  • Parent Pick-Up: Parents may collect students directly from the Comfest venue with prior permission.
📍 Destination: Seth Anandram Jaipuria School, Kanpur
🚨

Discipline & Code of Conduct

Strict discipline is mandatory. Disrespect, unfair means, or leaving campus leads to immediate disqualification.

  • ID Cards: Mandatory at all times on campus.
  • Campus Rules: Do not leave campus boundaries.
  • Punctuality: Report 10 mins early to event venues.
⚠️ Zero Tolerance for Misconduct or Unfair Means
💻

Device Allowance & Clashes

YES = Device permitted as per event rules. NO = Strictly no digital devices allowed.

  • Device Rule: Read event specifics before bringing equipment.
  • Event Clashes: Inform JCC in advance. Switching permitted ONLY if both events allow devices.
ℹ️ Student Ushers are available on campus to guide you.

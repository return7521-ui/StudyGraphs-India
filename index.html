READ BEFORE REGISTERING

Registration & Fee

  • If interested in ANY event, submit the Google Form at the bottom of this page.

Discipline & Conduct

  • Maintain strict discipline and behave respectfully.
  • Do not engage in inappropriate, disruptive or uncultural activities.
  • Follow instructions from organizers and ushers. Misbehaviour results in disqualification.

Campus Rules

  • Remain within the host-school campus. Do not leave except in emergencies.
  • Report to your event venue at least 10 minutes in advance.
  • Carry your required COMFEST identity card whenever applicable.

IMPORTANT — REGISTRATION DOES NOT CONFIRM SELECTION

Submitting the participation form does not mean that you are a confirmed candidate for COMFEST’26. The form is only an expression of your interest and will be used to understand students’ event preferences, skills, and participation interests.

After reviewing all submissions, the best-suited students from Sir Syed Public School, Unnao will be selected to represent the school. Selection will be based primarily on excellence and perfection in the chosen event, while also considering multi-talented students who demonstrate strong abilities across multiple events.

The final selection and allocation of students will be managed by Daksh, Shashank, and their team. Their decision regarding the final participants and event allocation will be considered final.

Filling the form carefully and honestly is therefore important, but it should not be considered a confirmation of participation.

Submit the form only if you are seriously interested.

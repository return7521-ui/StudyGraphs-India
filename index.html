IMPORTANT — PLEASE READ BEFORE REGISTERING

Please read these official participation instructions carefully. Submitting the interest form implies you are ready to adhere to these rules.

Registration & Cost

  • If you are interested in participating in ANY COMFEST event (one or multiple), submit the Google Form provided on this website.
  • Participation in COMFEST is completely FREE.
  • Only genuine/interested students should submit the form. Do not submit casually.

Discipline & Conduct

  • Students must maintain strict discipline and behave properly.
  • Do not engage in inappropriate, indecent, disruptive or uncultural activities.
  • Participants must follow instructions from JCC, ushers, and organizers.
  • Misbehaviour or violation of rules may result in immediate disqualification.

Campus & Travel

  • Remain within the host-school campus. Do not leave except in emergencies.
  • On event days, the school bus takes students to COMFEST and brings them back. Return to school is expected around 8:00 PM.
  • After reaching school, students must arrange their own travel home.
  • Parents/guardians may pick up their child directly from the COMFEST venue.
Note: If you choose multiple events that clash, you must inform the Jaipuria Computer Club in advance. Scheduling conflicts are not the responsibility of JCC. Carry your COMFEST ID card at all times.

Important Rules For All

  • Participants must report to the venue at least 10 minutes in advance.
  • Participants registered for simultaneous events must inform JCC in advance.
  • Misbehaviour or unfair means may lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Judges' and JCC decisions are final and binding.
  • Participants may approach ushers with doubts before events.
  • Basic first-aid facilities are available on campus.

DISCIPLINE & RESPONSIBILITY

Serious misconduct or use of unfair means will lead to immediate disqualification.

Must Do:

  • Maintain discipline & report on time.
  • Carry identity card at all times.
  • Follow organizer & usher instructions immediately.
  • Respect everyone (judges, staff, peers).
  • Stay within permitted campus areas.

Strictly Prohibited:

  • Engaging in inappropriate or uncultural activities.
  • Using unfair means or cheating.
  • Disturbing ongoing events.

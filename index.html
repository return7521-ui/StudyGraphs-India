PARTICIPATION GUIDE

Please read these official instructions carefully. Submitting the form implies you are ready to adhere to these rules.

Registration is Free

  • ✓ Participation in COMFEST is completely FREE.
  • ✓ If interested in ANY event, submit the Google Form.
  • ✓ Only genuine students who are serious about participating should submit.

Discipline Code

  • ✓ Maintain strict discipline and behave respectfully with everyone.
  • ✓ Do not engage in inappropriate or uncultural activities.
  • ✕ Misbehaviour or unfair means results in immediate disqualification.

Why Participate?

  • ★ Compete with students from other schools in a live environment.
  • ★ Explore tech, logic, art, debate, and business.
  • ★ Experience a large, prestigious student-organized festival.

Travel & Return Info

  • The return journey from COMFEST to school is expected to take until around 8:00 PM.
  • After reaching school, students will have to make their own arrangements to travel back home.
  • Parent Pick-up Option: A parent/guardian may pick up their child directly from the COMFEST venue.

Campus Rules

  • Do not leave the campus except in permitted/emergency circumstances.
  • Report to your event venue at least 10 minutes before the event starts.
  • Carry your required COMFEST identity card whenever applicable.
  • Basic first-aid facilities are available on campus.
